Questions

Does my audio leave my device?

No. Whisper runs in your browser via WebAssembly. The only network request is the one-time model download (~100 MB), cached permanently. Your audio is never transmitted anywhere.

Why does the first run take longer?

The Whisper Small English model (~100 MB) is downloaded once from Hugging Face, then cached in your browser. Subsequent runs start immediately.

How accurate is it?

Very accurate for clear English speech. Whisper Small achieves under 5% word error rate on standard benchmarks. Background noise or low-quality recordings will reduce accuracy. You can edit the result directly in the transcript panel.

What other formats work?

Optimised for OGG (.ogg, .oga). The browser's audio decoder also handles MP3, WAV, M4A, and FLAC on most modern browsers.
