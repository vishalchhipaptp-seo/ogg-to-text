Questions

Does my audio leave my device?

No. The Whisper model runs in your browser using WebAssembly. The only network request is the one-time model download (~100 MB from Hugging Face CDN), which is then cached. Your audio file is never transmitted anywhere.

Why does the first run take longer?

The Whisper Small English model (~100 MB) is downloaded from Hugging Face the first time. After that it lives in your browser cache, and subsequent runs start immediately.

How accurate is it?

Very accurate for clear English speech — Whisper Small achieves word error rates under 5% on standard benchmarks. Background noise, heavy accents, or low-quality recordings will reduce accuracy. You can always edit the result directly in the text area.

What other formats work?

Optimised for OGG (.ogg, .oga). The browser's built-in audio decoder also handles MP3, WAV, M4A, and FLAC on most modern browsers — just drag one in.
